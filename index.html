How it works

Three steps to dictate anything

No setup, no account, no internet after the first model download.

1

Install & download a model

Run the installer. On first launch, pick a Whisper model size that suits your needs — it downloads in minutes.

2

Press the hotkey

Hit Ctrl+Alt+Space from anywhere in Windows. The floating overlay appears at the top of your screen.

3

Speak, then click to insert

Click the mic to start, speak naturally, click again to stop. Your text is inserted exactly where your cursor was.

4

Works in any app

VS Code, Notion, Word, Chrome, WhatsApp Web — anywhere you can type, Akoway can dictate.
