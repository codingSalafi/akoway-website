How it works

Three steps to dictate anything

No setup, no account, no internet after the first model download.

1

Install & pick a model

Run the installer. On first launch, pick a speech model — Bark 3.0 Clean (240 MB, recommended) or Bark 3.0 Pro (910 MB) — and Akoway downloads it once.

2

Press the hotkey

Hit Ctrl+Alt+F9 from anywhere in Windows. The floating overlay appears at the top of your screen.

3

Speak, then click to insert

Click the mic to start, speak naturally, click again to stop. Your text is inserted exactly where your cursor was.

4

Works in any app

VS Code, Notion, Word, Chrome, WhatsApp Web — anywhere you can type, Akoway can dictate.

Speech Models

Two models: word for word, or cleaned up

Pick one on first launch and switch any time in Settings. Both are open models by Underdog, downloaded once — nothing bundled in the installer.

Model Size Speed (CPU) Notes
Bark 3.0 Pro 910 MB ~15× faster than real time Most accurate. Writes exactly what you say, with punctuation & capitals
Bark 3.0 Clean Recommended 240 MB ~4× faster than real time Removes ums, repeats and false starts as it writes
